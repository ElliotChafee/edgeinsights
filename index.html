I attended North Palos School District 117 (NPD) — a district that proves data can tell an incredible story.

NPD's schools consistently exceed expectations, earning six National Blue Ribbon Awards since 2012. This achievement stands out even more knowing that 64% of students are low-income and 44% are English Learners.

Beyond academics, NPD demonstrates strong financial stewardship. By partnering with the State to rewrite Illinois' school funding formula, the district increased annual state aid from $4 million to $30 million annually.

This financial transformation allowed NPD to:

  • Invest $80 million in school expansion and modernization — without raising property taxes.
  • Provide $15 million in property tax relief to local residents.
